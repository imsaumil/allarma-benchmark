About

Citation

Benchmarking aLLarMa: A Constrained Two-Stage GraphRAG Framework for Power System Alarm, Network and Operational Data Analytics

Authors: Saumil Shah (NC State University; Electric Power Research Institute), Adrian Kelly (Electric Power Research Institute, Ireland), Wenyuan Tang (NC State University)

Predecessor (CIGRE 2026 Paris Session, Paper ID 12538): 3-model subset dashboard

Methodology: 548 audited production eval logs across 9 open-weight LLMs (0.8B–20B) and two machines.

Hardware

  • SKORGE (consumer-grade workstation): AMD Ryzen 9 7950X CPU · NVIDIA RTX 4090 GPU (24 GB) · 128 GB RAM · vLLM serving.
  • NVIDIA DGX Spark (self-contained 240 W edge-class appliance): GB10 Grace–Blackwell superchip (20-core ARM CPU + 5th-gen Tensor-Core GPU, 128 GB unified memory) · vLLM serving.
